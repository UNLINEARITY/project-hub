Compose, then make room.

Start a composition or review your library. Picliva remembers where you stopped; you choose what to keep and confirm deletions through the system.

Keep the motion.

Combine stills and Live Photos, preview the motion, and save the result as a new Live Photo. At least one Live Photo is needed; keeping its original audio is part of Pro.

Your originals stay yours.

Compositions are processed on your device, and saving creates a new result. There are no ads or analytics SDKs, and your media is not uploaded to developer servers. Apple system services handle purchases, restoration, and iCloud access, which may require a connection.
