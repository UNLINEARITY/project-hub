Here to help.

Picliva joins your photos into one image and helps you make room in your library. These are the answers we send most often — and you can always write to us.

Restore Picliva Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Sign in with the Apple Account that bought it, open Picliva, go to Settings and choose Restore Purchases. If nothing comes back, make sure you are online and that the App Store is reachable, then try again.

What Pro includes

Four things: creative compositions with nested layouts, cropping a single photo or a boundary, keeping Live Photo audio in exports, and video review in your library.

Everything else is free, with no watermark and no export limit — Vertical and horizontal stitching, importing, reordering, spacing, JPEG or PNG export at standard, high or full resolution, Live Photo compositions and the entire library review flow.

Photo access

Picliva asks for access to your library and you can change it at any time in system Settings → Picliva → Photos. Media kept in iCloud may need a connection and a little time to load through Apple’s services.

Save a composition

When the composition looks right, save it to Photos or export a file. A Live Photo composition needs at least one Live Photo, and keeping its sound is part of Pro. Saving always adds a new item: your originals keep their own pixels, metadata and Live Photo motion.

Review and delete in the library

Swipe through the library and choose what to keep or delete. Picliva remembers where you stopped. Single mode asks for system confirmation for each item; batch mode marks items while you review and asks for confirmation when you finish. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in Photos, where Apple’s retention rules apply.

Purchases and refunds

Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. For a refund, use Apple’s report a problem page; we cannot issue one from here. Restore Purchases is the way to bring Pro back on another device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Write to us

Email unlinearity@gmail.com and include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the problem. We read every message and answer in English, Chinese or Japanese.

Please never send passwords, payment details, or private photos you would not want kept in an email. For anything else, the privacy policy explains what the app does and does not do with your media.
