### Event Horizon Timer

Work quietly, then let a black hole remind you to rest. Set your own work and rest durations, choose a black-hole style, and explore the built-in interactive science pages.

The core timer, two styles, and today’s statistics are free. Optional Pro is a one-time purchase for all nine styles, appearance adjustments, and historical statistics. Requires a Metal-capable Mac. Preparing for the Mac App Store.

[Product and screenshots](https://unlinearity.github.io/project-hub/event-horizon/) · [Support](https://unlinearity.github.io/project-hub/event-horizon/support.html) · [Privacy](https://unlinearity.github.io/project-hub/event-horizon/privacy.html)

### Picliva

Turn photos and Live Photos into one image on your iPhone, then make room in your library. Vertical and horizontal stitching, full-resolution JPEG and PNG export, and photo library review are free, with no watermark or export limit.

Optional one-time Pro adds creative layouts, cropping, Live Photo audio, and video review. Available in English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese.
